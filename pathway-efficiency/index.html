A QUESTION FOR YOUR SERVICE

Could richer measurement at an existing patient touchpoint remove unnecessary steps elsewhere?

That is an opportunity to investigate, not a saving to assume.

For private hospitals, healthcare groups, operations directors, service managers and clinical leads, the starting point is the existing pathway. Who is already seeing the patient? What information is available? What would a body composition result change?

A short measurement can still require preparation, assistance, cleaning, clinical interpretation and a conversation. Map the complete process before judging operational value.

Define a hypothesis you can test

For example: “Adding appropriate measurement to an existing review may improve information available to the clinician without increasing total patient touchpoints.” Record the baseline, observe the pilot and report what actually happens.

24 seconds is not a 24-second appointment

seca publishes a 24-second BIA measurement time. This planner does not turn that into appointment capacity. Preparation, eligibility checks, cleaning, assistance, consultation and interpretation time must be assessed separately.

How the planner works

Annual measurements = patients per year × measurements per patient. Site, working-day and device averages divide that total by the corresponding input. Device-day workload divides the annual total by both devices and working days. No appointment reduction, staffing reduction or financial return is calculated.
