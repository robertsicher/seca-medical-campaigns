MORE THAN A NUMBER

A measurement.
A more informed conversation.

Two people with similar weight or BMI can have different body composition.

Looking at fat mass and skeletal muscle gives a different perspective from total bodyweight alone. The useful question is what that information adds to an assessment, a treatment review or a patient conversation.

Alongside clinical assessment

Body composition does not replace medical history, examination, functional assessment or appropriate clinical tests. It is not a standalone diagnosis.

THE ENABLING TECHNOLOGY

More insight from every measurement.

seca mBCA Alpha

Medical body composition analysis, built around the questions behind the weight.

seca lists the mBCA Alpha as an MDR-certified medical device using 8-point bioelectrical impedance analysis. Results include skeletal muscle mass, fat mass, visceral fat and phase angle.

24 seconds
Published BIA measurement time, not the total clinical process.
8-point BIA
Hand and foot electrode measurement.
Analytics 125
Browser-based review of results and changes over time.
myAnalytics
Patient access to results, when enabled by the provider.

seca describes validation against whole-body MRI for skeletal muscle mass and a four-compartment model for fat-free mass. These are parameter-specific reference methods, not interchangeable clinical tests.

Official product details ↗

seca UK: mBCA Alpha · seca UK: Analytics 125 · seca UK: myAnalytics · seca UK: clinical validation

Is it appropriate for your patients?

Check the current instructions for use, contraindications and your cohort before measurement. seca lists an age range of 5–99 years, height above 130 cm and a 300 kg capacity. This adult-focused campaign does not establish suitability for every patient or every clinical condition.
