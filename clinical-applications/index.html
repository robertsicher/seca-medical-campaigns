Weight management

What is changing during weight loss?

How do fat and skeletal muscle change across a treatment programme?

What bodyweight alone doesn’t show
Total kilograms do not separate tissue changes or fluid variation.
Potentially useful parameters
Fat mass, skeletal muscle mass and selected context-appropriate parameters.
Role of repeat measurement
Compare with a baseline under comparable conditions at clinically meaningful reviews.
How mBCA Alpha could fit
Consider an mBCA Alpha measurement in an existing review, with eligibility and interpretation agreed.

Wilding et al. (2021): STEP 1 exploratory DXA analysis · seca UK: mBCA Alpha

Clinical nutrition

Make nutritional change measurable.

What additional information could support a nutritional assessment or review?

What bodyweight alone doesn’t show
Weight alone does not describe the balance of muscle, fat and fluid.
Potentially useful parameters
Skeletal muscle mass, fat mass and, where relevant, phase angle or water-related parameters.
Role of repeat measurement
Record preparation, illness and fluid context; avoid attributing every change to nutrition.
How mBCA Alpha could fit
Use appropriate results as one input to a dietitian-led assessment, not a replacement for nutritional screening.

Healthy ageing

When stable weight doesn’t mean nothing has changed.

Could muscle quantity have changed despite little change in bodyweight?

What bodyweight alone doesn’t show
A stable total can conceal offsetting changes in different body components.
Potentially useful parameters
Skeletal muscle mass, considered alongside strength, function and relevant clinical information.
Role of repeat measurement
Use comparable observations to support review, not an assumption of inevitable decline.
How mBCA Alpha could fit
Confirm standing ability, eligibility and the suitability of the method for the individual.

Cruz-Jentoft et al. (2019): EWGSOP2 consensus · seca UK: clinical validation

Rehabilitation

Measure the rebuild.

Could body composition add context during a rehabilitation programme?

What bodyweight alone doesn’t show
Bodyweight does not show muscle distribution, strength or functional recovery.
Potentially useful parameters
Total and segmental skeletal muscle estimates, where appropriate to the clinical question.
Role of repeat measurement
Interpret trends with functional assessment, symptoms and the wider rehabilitation plan.
How mBCA Alpha could fit
Only use a standing device when safe and suitable. Do not equate changes in estimates with restored function.

Sports medicine

Context beyond bodyweight.

What information complements the athlete’s clinical and functional assessment?

What bodyweight alone doesn’t show
Weight does not distinguish body components or demonstrate recovery from injury.
Potentially useful parameters
Skeletal muscle, segmental distribution and fat mass, selected for a clinical purpose.
Role of repeat measurement
Standardise timing around exercise, food and hydration; use the same method where possible.
How mBCA Alpha could fit
Combine suitable measurement with professional interpretation. Avoid universal body-fat or muscle targets.

seca UK: medical body composition applications · seca UK: myAnalytics

Preventative healthcare

A broader baseline for a health conversation.

Could additional information make an appropriate health assessment more informative?

What bodyweight alone doesn’t show
BMI does not directly describe skeletal muscle or the distribution of fat.
Potentially useful parameters
Fat mass, skeletal muscle and visceral fat estimates, interpreted with other risk information.
Role of repeat measurement
Use follow-up when it answers a relevant clinical question, not simply to create a recurring test.
How mBCA Alpha could fit
Position mBCA Alpha alongside appropriate history, examination and tests, not as a disease screen.

NICE NG246: overweight and obesity management · seca UK: mBCA Alpha

CLINICAL JUDGEMENT REMAINS CENTRAL

A measurement input.
Not a standalone diagnosis.

ELIGIBILITY

Start with suitability

Review the current instructions for use, contraindications, physical requirements and patient cohort.

INTERPRETATION

Consider the whole assessment

Sarcopenia assessment includes strength and function. Body composition alone does not establish the diagnosis.

EVIDENCE

Ask which claim the study supports

Method validation does not prove that installing a device improves outcomes within a particular service.
