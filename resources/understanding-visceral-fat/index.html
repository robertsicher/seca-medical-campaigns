01

Where is visceral fat?

Visceral fat is located inside the abdominal cavity, around internal organs. It differs from subcutaneous fat, which lies beneath the skin. Total weight or BMI does not directly describe this distribution.

seca UK: Analytics 125 · seca: understanding body composition results (PDF)

02

What does body composition analysis add?

seca lists visceral fat among the available mBCA Alpha parameters. The result is an estimate from body composition analysis, not a direct image of the abdomen. Ask which parameter, units and reference information are being reported.

seca UK: mBCA Alpha

03

How should it be discussed?

Explain the result alongside the patient’s clinical history, examination and other appropriate information. Do not use it alone to diagnose disease or present a single reading as a complete risk assessment.

Where repeat measurements are considered, establish why the comparison matters and how it will be interpreted. Avoid universal treatment targets or alarming language based on one number.

04

Which next question is useful?

For an individual review, ask what this parameter adds to the conversation. For a service considering deployment, ask whether clinicians find it relevant, understandable and practical to use within an existing assessment.
