BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

Generate demand from genuine healthcare need.

Do not rely solely on people searching for a product they may not know exists.

Reach organisations through the clinical, patient, commercial and operational questions they are already asking. Establish the problem and value before introducing mBCA Alpha as the enabling technology.

The campaign connects problem-led organic search, useful clinical resources, relevant nurture and coordinated account engagement to demonstrations, pilots, opportunities and long-term customer relationships.

Product claims require current official evidence. Proposed service benefits are hypotheses to test. Clinical review, brand approval, data governance and commercial validation are launch requirements.

Identity is not the same as intent

A resource conversion creates an opportunity to identify a relevant professional and organisation. An MQL should require agreed fit and intent, with explicit requests routed promptly to a human regardless of their numerical score.

HUBSPOT & PROGRESSIVE PROFILING

Ask enough to be relevant.
Earn the next question.

FIRST CONVERSION

A short initial form

First name, last name, work email, organisation and clinical area. Areas: weight management, nutrition, healthy ageing, rehabilitation, private health, NHS and other.

LATER CONVERSIONS

Add organisational context

Job role, organisation type, number of sites, approximate patient volume, current body composition provision and primary objective. Request these only when relevant.

CONTACT + COMPANY

One organisational record

Associate people with the correct organisation. Validate domains and organisation names. NHS shared domains and healthcare groups may require human review rather than automatic one-domain assumptions.

Prototype behaviour

All forms are simulated. No field values are transmitted, stored, logged or sent to HubSpot. Use fictional details. A next-stage form can be previewed in the same page session; the prototype retains only an in-memory demonstration state, not a personal profile.

Live integration considerations

Use approved HubSpot forms, privacy information and consent controls appropriate to the communication. Confirm the account’s available features and configuration. Keep patient records and measurement data out of marketing automation.

Resource requests and marketing subscriptions must not be silently treated as the same permission. Apply suppression, unsubscribe handling, retention and human review.

Illustrative scoring only

Actual weighting should be calibrated against seca’s sales conversion data. Set score caps, recency and decay rules. Exclude staff, test traffic and repeated event inflation. Agree qualification and disqualification rules with sales rather than inventing an MQL threshold.

Webinars are a proposed nurture asset, not a scheduled event offered by this prototype. High-intent requests should trigger timely, relevant human follow-up. A high score alone is not permission to contact someone.

Agree lifecycle definitions, conversion denominators, attribution windows and account ownership. Distinguish sourced pipeline from influenced pipeline, deduplicate opportunities and reconcile won revenue with sales records. Review conversion by clinical area and company, not just by channel.
