## Verified product facts used

- seca UK lists mBCA Alpha as MDR certified and using 8-point bioelectrical impedance analysis.
- Published BIA measurement period: 24 seconds. Never treated as total appointment, cleaning, preparation or interpretation time.
- Officially identified parameters include skeletal muscle mass, fat mass, visceral fat and phase angle.
- Analytics 125 supports result evaluation and visualisation; myAnalytics supports provider-enabled patient access.
- Reference methods are parameter-specific: whole-body MRI for skeletal muscle and the four-compartment model for fat-free mass.
- The official product page lists 5–99 years, height >130 cm and 300 kg capacity. These technical limits do not establish suitability for every patient.
- seca describes extended validation in relevant population groups. This prototype avoids blanket suitability statements and avoids repeating promotional accuracy percentages as individual measurement guarantees.

## Clinical boundaries

- BIA provides estimates; it is not direct tissue imaging.
- BMI is retained as an established assessment measure. NICE NG246 is referenced; the campaign does not recommend substituting BIA for BMI.
- Lean mass / fat-free mass is not synonymous with skeletal muscle.
- The STEP 1 resource is an exploratory DXA analysis, not an Alpha outcomes trial. No treatment recommendation is made.
- Sarcopenia is not diagnosed independently by Alpha here. The EWGSOP2 distinction between strength, muscle quantity/quality and performance is preserved.
- Phase angle is not presented as a standalone diagnosis or universal health score.
- No device-led weight-loss, recovery, retention, appointment reduction, NHS savings or improved patient-outcome claim is made.
- Pathway and commercial benefits are hypotheses requiring local evaluation.
- No device purchase price is invented.
- The campaign is adult-focused. Current instructions for use, contraindications and cohort-specific evidence must be checked before any clinical deployment.
